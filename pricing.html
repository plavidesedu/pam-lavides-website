Cancellations

Cancellations & missed classes

I understand that life happens, so I keep my policy simple and flexible.

  • Cancel at least 12 hours before class — Free, anytime. Your class goes back into your package.
  • Late cancellations (less than 12 hours) — You get 2 free late cancellations each month. From the 3rd late cancellation in the same month, the class will count as used.
  • Missed classes without a message — The class will count as used. Even a quick message right before class helps, and it counts as a late cancellation instead.
  • Running late — Classes end at the scheduled time. If I don't hear from you within 15 minutes, the class counts as missed.
  • Emergencies — Illness, family emergencies, natural disasters, and power or internet outages never count against you. Just let me know when you can.
My promise to you

If I ever need to cancel with less than 12 hours' notice, you won't lose anything. You'll get first choice of a make-up time. If I miss a class without notice, I'll add one free class to your package.
