Targeted Solutions for Your English Goals

I don't believe in one-size-fits-all learning. Whether you are aiming for a career breakthrough, preparing for an international move, or simply want to stop feeling "stuck" when you speak, I have a course designed for you. Teaching since 2015, I use my experience to curate the best free, high-quality resources from across the internet — articles, podcasts, and real-world videos — saving you the cost of expensive textbooks while keeping your lessons fresh and relevant.

Courses

Conversational Fluency

Daily conversation practice built around real topics — designed to help you think and respond naturally in English, not just recite phrases.

Who it's for
Intermediate learners who understand English but freeze, or translate in their heads, when they speak.
What a lesson looks like
We talk about real topics from your life and work → I note your mistakes and better phrases as we go → we review them together at the end.
The result
You answer faster and more naturally, without translating in your head.

Free Conversation

Relaxed, open-ended sessions for students who mainly want mileage: talking, listening, and building confidence without a rigid structure.

Who it's for
Students of any level who want more speaking time — busy professionals, or learners keeping their English active.
What a lesson looks like
You pick the topic, or I suggest one → we simply talk → I share gentle corrections and new vocabulary at the end.
The result
More ease and confidence in speaking, through regular practice that fits your schedule.

Pronunciation Practice

Focused work on rhythm, intonation, and the specific sounds that are hardest for your native language — the "textbook to real world" gap.

Who it's for
Learners of any level who are understood but want clearer, more natural English — especially Chinese, Japanese and Korean speakers.
What a lesson looks like
We pick the sounds, stress and intonation that are hardest for you → we practice them together → I correct you and we review.
The result
Clearer, more natural speech, so people understand you the first time.

IELTS Preparation

Structured prep covering all four sections, with strategies and practice drawn from real test experience.

Who it's for
Students preparing for IELTS for study, work or migration, at intermediate level or above.
What a lesson looks like
We practice one section at a time (Speaking, Writing, Reading or Listening) with test-style tasks → I explain how answers are scored → you get feedback on how to improve.
The result
You know what the test expects and how to raise your score. I have examiner-related training, so I know what examiners look for.
