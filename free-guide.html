10 Phrases to Sound Natural in Job Interviews

Thank you! Here is your free guide. Practice saying each example out loud, then change it to fit your own job and experience.

Phrases
  • 1. Thank you for having me. — Start warmly and politely.
    “Good morning, and thank you for having me. I'm excited to be here.”
  • 2. I'd be happy to walk you through my background. — A smooth way to answer “Tell me about yourself.”
    “I'd be happy to walk you through my background. I've worked in marketing for five years.”
  • 3. One of my key strengths is… — Talk about strengths without sounding arrogant.
    “One of my key strengths is explaining complex ideas in simple terms.”
  • 4. In my previous role, I was responsible for… — Describe your past work clearly.
    “In my previous role, I was responsible for managing a team of four engineers.”
  • 5. I took the initiative to… — Show that you act without being told.
    “I took the initiative to create a new onboarding guide for our team.”
  • 6. A challenge I faced was… Here's how I handled it. — Answer “Tell me about a difficult situation.”
    “A challenge I faced was a tight deadline. Here's how I handled it: I broke the project into small steps and asked for help early.”
  • 7. I work well under pressure. — Show you stay calm and reliable.
    “I work well under pressure. I stay organized and focus on priorities.”
  • 8. As a result, we were able to… — Finish a story with a clear result.
    “As a result, we were able to deliver the project two days early.”
  • 9. Could you tell me more about…? — Ask for more detail, or buy time to think.
    “Could you tell me more about the team I would be working with?”
  • 10. I'm excited about this opportunity because… — Close with confidence and a reason.
    “I'm excited about this opportunity because it matches my skills and my long-term goals. Thank you for your time.”
Tip

Don't memorize whole answers. Learn these phrases as building blocks, then add your own details so your answers sound natural and honest.
